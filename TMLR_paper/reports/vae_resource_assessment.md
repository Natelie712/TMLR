# Figure 2 and VAE resource/provenance audit

The model-independent synthetic experiment remains scientifically distinct from LLM activation geometry. `evals/toy_models_steering.py` uses a 10-dimensional hypersphere in ambient512, radius10/curvature, noise0.01 and seed42, matching key manuscript ingredients. However its checked-in executable grid spans strength0–10, while the manuscript states0–20. No matching frozen numerical archive was located in the project results search. Therefore the old synthetic panels are eligible for retention after author/provenance confirmation, but are not certified byte-for-byte or numerically reproduced here. They are not automatically discarded because CAA replaces behavioral data.

The VAE pullback implementation is in `notebooks/learn_geometry/exp_03_02_geometry_of_activations_with_vae_pullback-cleaned_betterrunid_betterhistlogging.ipynb` and related exp03 notebooks. The inspected active setup uses latent dimension2, 10 ensemble members, batch64, 10 epochs, learning rate3e-4, decay1e-4, KL warmup0.3, beta_max1 and gradient clipping1. Some functions/defaults use50 epochs and500 geodesic optimization iterations; these differences need an explicit future freeze. The notebook also contains WandB/upload calls, which are not executed by this revision.

`scripts/analysis/compute_geodesic_analysis.py` computes kNN graph detour distances. It is not the manuscript's VAE ensemble pullback method and cannot be relabelled as Figure2B reproduction. No compatible frozen VAE checkpoint with the three current model revisions, selected blocks and CAA fit IDs was located. Raw activations can be reused; old learned VAE weights cannot be assumed compatible.

Cost estimate by operation count: a 21-cell, 10-member, 10-epoch experiment entails 210 VAE models and roughly tens of thousands of optimizer steps at batch64. At500 sampled paths and500 path-optimization steps per cell, the geodesic stage entails5.25 million path optimizer iterations, each potentially evaluating10 decoders/Jacobians. This stage can dominate training by orders of magnitude. A reliable wall-clock estimate is unavailable without a bounded benchmark; no invented GPU-hour figure is reported.

Proposed reduced-scope cost pilot, NOT launched: Qwen Hallucination and Survival Instinct, training-only standardized data, 3 members ×10 epochs, 20 validation endpoint pairs ×50 path steps. This tests runtime/memory and reconstruction calibration, not the original ten-member/five-hundred-pair protocol. Measure time per training step and per path, then extrapolate with uncertainty before author approval of a full distortion study. A second model may be added only after that cost assessment.

The manuscript compares a pullback length in decoder-induced units to Euclidean distance in latent coordinates. Decoder scaling and latent reparameterization affect that ratio; a large ratio alone is not a scale-invariant proof of intrinsic curvature or of steering effectiveness. Resolve the metric/denominator and ensemble uncertainty conventions before using new curvature claims. For the present CAA revision, omit unverified empirical Figure2B conclusions and any claimed curvature–steering causal link. No VAE or external judge has been launched.
